Only for the standard Waveshare ESP32-S3 Touch AMOLED 1.75-inch model. Do not flash 1.43-inch, 1.75C, or ESP32-C3 boards.

Instructions

  1. Plug your board into your Windows PC with a USB-C data cable.
  2. Open this webpage in desktop Chrome or Edge.
  3. Click Connect & install and select the right COM port.
  4. After flashing, press RESET without holding BOOT.

If a serial port is unavailable, hold BOOT, briefly press RESET, release BOOT, and retry.
